WHAT S4 ESTABLISHES (unchanged from s4-claude-review.txt, now with the fixes)
- One entry, Runtime.execute. It interprets the actual compiled P1 graph through the pure,
  fuel-free Machine, with every node form implemented and covered by both
  interpreter_denotation and graph_contract_execution.
- Deterministic route, for every request, checked P1 operation and agreeing adapter:
  - the response equals EvalSpec, so it is authorized and conforms;
  - it is error-free under SuccessConditions;
  - filtering the snapshot to the caller's tenant leaves it unchanged (modeled response
    values only).
- Fault route: for every pure or stateful driver whose answers are exact success or declared
  framework failure, the response is AuthorizedResponse and Conforms.
  - The proof uses local per-constructor evidence, explicit occurrence positions and derived
    ownership, with no EvalSpec equality.
  - Equal rows may receive different answers.
- A tenant-free items(first:) length bound, obtained from the contract through the same Sat
  theorem and preserved through completion at the selected path.
