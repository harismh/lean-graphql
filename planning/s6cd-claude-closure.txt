WHAT S6 ESTABLISHES (closure scope)
- For a dynamically submitted P2 request on the real LeanIO route, the returned bytes are one
  of two things.
  - A closed public failure, encoded in the independent JSON grammar, with a status that
    matches its failure.
    - Preparation failures (media, method, content type, body and query limits, JSON and
      envelope, parse, operation name, validation, variables) happen before any
      authentication, acquisition or backend effect.
    - Every caught exception is redacted.
  - Or, under the same caller credential:
    - successful preparation;
    - AuthSound(credential, principal);
    - AcquireSound(principal, snapshot);
    - independent variable and key meanings;
    - bytes that UTF-8-decode to an ordered-JSON text encoding a structured response, which
      is AuthorizedResponse for exactly that snapshot and principal under the
      key-instantiated selection.
- That response comes from the actual S5 dispatcher and the S4b optimized Machine through
  S6a's certified transport driver and S6b's universal fault Sat. Per-key faults and
  whole-batch transport failure are kept distinct, and errors appear at their original
  coordinates.

TRUST BOUNDARY (accurately stated in the status documents)
- The text decoders are tested, not proved: Lean.Json.parse, checkEnvelope and decode,
  parseOperation, and HTTP header and body extraction.
- AuthSound, AcquireSound and backend refinement for any non-demo producer. The demo bearer
  allowlist and the fixed store are test policy only.
- Native runtime, LeanIO, sockets, delivery, termination, liveness, scheduling, cancellation
  and resource bounds.
- Routes and methods other than /graphql POST/GET use LeanIO defaults, outside the
  certificate.
- Optimized deduplication shares permitted faults across equal keys (S6b L4).
- Generic wave compatibility is for all vectors. Refine it before nontrivial compatibility
  is introduced (S6b L1, recorded for S7 and S8).
- P3 features are not enabled. S7 is the next slice.
