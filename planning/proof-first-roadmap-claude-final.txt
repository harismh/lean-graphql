and literals, so it can name unknown fields or a scalar with children. Either define a validity

decrement points into the specification and compromise its independence. Recommended: keep EvalSpec
free of fuel and faults, require exact agreement at sufficient fuel, and cover low fuel with the
every-fuel safety theorem (L356). L582 reads consistently under this choice.

D3. Framework failures in AuthTree. L356 and L454 need exhaustion and InternalError to be accepted
at any field, and argument errors at their field. Include them in S0's failure rules, so that S6
can return a caught exception without reopening AuthTree.

latter as the instantiated selection obtained from the operation and coerced variables, which also
gives S5 its bridge (L403-404). This can be written in S0 or S5.
