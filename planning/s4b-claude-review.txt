VERDICT: S4b MAY CLOSE. THERE ARE NO BLOCKERS AND NO HIGH FINDINGS.
- The transformed path has a whole structured-response equivalence theorem and inherits
  authorization and conformance.
- The selected stage has an exact, answer-independent primitive count of ceil(U/B), over actual
  .batch suspensions with nonempty chunks of at most B inputs.
- The acceptance examples are kernel-checked.
Two medium findings concern the boundaries of what the count and the safety theorems cover.
M1 is cheap and should be fixed before S5. M2 is an S6 obligation to record now.

FINDINGS
M1 (Medium) The reduced count is proved only for the isolated rowsWave stage. Nothing relates
   it to the program the dispatcher actually runs.
   - optimized_stage_count, the native --batch-benchmark (Demo.lean:6-14) and the JSON all
     count Batch.rowsWave in isolation.
   - No theorem says how many primitives Batching.optimized or Batching.execute issues, or
     that the only Task.project primitives issued for the prepared rows are the wave's
     ceil(U/B) batches.
   - A regression that kept rowsWave but also issued per-row Task.project singles in `finish`,
     or ran `sequential` and discarded its result, would leave every current theorem true:
     response equality still holds, and the stage count is unaffected.
   - The roadmap asks to "Instrument the actual Machine program emitted by the batch strategy"
     and to bound the calls "issued for that transformed load".
   Fix (small):
   - Prove a runCount_bind lemma.
   - Prove, for every resolver, a whole-path decomposition:
     runCount(optimized) = runCount(prepare) + ceil(U/B) + runCount(finish …), and
     runCount(reference) = runCount(prepare) + |positions| + runCount(finish …), with the same
     prepare and finish terms. U and |positions| are computed from that resolver's prepared
     value.
   - Derive the whole-path saving |positions| − ceil(U/B).
   - Make the benchmark also report the whole Batching.execute and Runtime.execute counts. My
     probe gives 9 and 12 on the fixture.
   Optionally, also prove that `finish` issues no Task.project call at the top level.
M2 (Medium, S6 boundary) The optimized path has no fault-safety (Sat) theorem. Its "ready
   input" facts are not used in any statement about the machine.
   - The status lists prepared_rows_owned, prepared_input_pre, representative_pre and
     task_batch_compatible as included (BatchCost.lean:30-56). They are stated only for
     preparedFor (Generated.adapter Generated.application), the pure deterministic run, and
     no theorem composes them.
   - There is no
     Sat Fault.contracts.Pre Fault.contracts.Post (Batching.execute …) AuthorizedResponse. So
     the precondition of the .batch call (compatible plus every representative owned) is not
     discharged for arbitrary permitted answers to prepare.
   - The demo now runs Batching.execute. The S4 fault-safety theorem
     Fault.execution_authorized covers only Runtime.execute.
   - The status says correctly that faulting execution is not reordered and that the
     sequential entry remains. The roadmap makes fault safety "separate", so this does not
     block S4b.
   Fix:
   (a) Narrow the status. These are pure-run ingredient lemmas, not a proved precondition of
       the optimized machine.
   (b) Record an explicit S6 obligation. Before any handler drives Batching.execute, prove Sat
       over Fault.contracts for the optimized machine:
       - rows from permitted prepare answers are owned, because exact success keeps the domain
         rows;
       - representatives are members of those rows;
       - compatibility holds;
       - a per-position exact-or-framework batch answer reconstructs through the S4 local fault
         evidence.
       Also prove that a whole-batch transport failure is a distinct, declared outcome. It must
       not be scattered as per-key failures.
L1 (Low) Equality at the dispatcher level needs BindingAgreement, while the stage equivalence
   does not.
   - execute_eq_runtime connects the reconstructed `checked child nonempty` to the caller's
     `selected` through EvalSpec (BatchExecution.lean:70-72), so it needs agreement.
   - The roadmap states equivalence for any deterministic backend whose capability is exact
     and keyed. optimized_runtime meets that only for the reconstructed checked operation.
   Fix: prove that IR.Resolved (or IR.Checked) is unique for a given surface selection. Then
   state execute_eq_runtime for every adapter whose taskProject capability is eligible, with
   no agreement, and keep the agreeing version as a corollary.
L2 (Low) The negative fixtures test values, not the implementation.
   - BatchWrongScatter compares a constant function with twoRows.getValue on a standalone
     Gathered (S4bProofs.lean:68-75). It does not change Batch.position or scatterResults in
     the actual wave.
   - BatchCrossScope refutes cross-request equality of the backend, not an obligation of the
     batching API. As noted under Scope, mixing requests inside one batch is already
     unrepresentable, which is the stronger guarantee.
   - BatchZeroCapacity only shows ¬(0 < 0).
   Fix: add a wrong-position wave variant, for example position replaced by a constant 0, and
   show that wave_exact fails or that per_occurrence_errors changes on the fixture. The same
   pattern was used for S3's unguardedAdapter and S4's swallowedExecution.
L3 (Low) The demo prints a fixed claim.
   - "5 single calls -> 2 batch calls (3 distinct keys, capacity 2)" (Demo.lean:19) is a
     literal string. It is not computed in the default run, though the theorems and the
     benchmark mode do back it.
   Fix: compute and check the counts as the benchmark mode does, or drop the numbers.
L4 (Low, wording) Recognition limits.
   - recognizeShape matches literal field names with empty argument lists
     (BatchExecution.lean:36-43). The reconstructed child comes from IR.lower and is checked
     again. Nested loads inside the child are not optimized.
   The status states all of this. Note also that Eligible is evidence for the reconstructed
   operation, linked to `selected` only by `shape : surface = selection nested`. That is enough
   given L1's uniqueness lemma.

OBSERVATIONS
- The capability's run is the pointwise loop (Bindings.lean:120-127). So the primitive count is
  a model of requests, and no backend work is saved today. The status says this correctly.
- The structure matches the original goal: the plan is checked, and the shared computation is
  scattered back to every response occurrence through the unchanged compiled child graph and
  output plan. The optimization is a specialized lowering to Machine, not a rewrite of Graph
  syntax, and the status says so honestly.
